Australian Homeowners Are Taking a Closer Look at Solar as Electricity Bills Put Pressure on Household Budgets

For many Australian homeowners, electricity bills have become an important part of managing monthly household expenses. And with a rooftop of their own, some homeowners are beginning to explore whether solar energy could be a practical option for their property.

But before investing in a solar system, there is one important question to consider: Is your home suitable for solar, and what options might be available to you?

Why Are More Homeowners Exploring Solar?

When a large portion of the household budget goes toward electricity, it makes sense to explore alternatives. A suitable solar system may help some households reduce their reliance on electricity from the grid, depending on their energy usage, property, system costs, and local conditions.

However, not every property is the same. Roof condition, shading, electricity consumption, and installation requirements can all affect whether solar is a suitable option.

That is why checking your home's eligibility is a sensible place to start.

A Simple First Step: Check Your Solar Eligibility

Rather than making assumptions, homeowners can begin by providing a few basic details about their property and electricity usage. This information can help determine whether their home may be suitable for solar and whether any relevant installation or financing options are available.

This eligibility check is intended for homeowners who meet the following criteria:

Eligibility criteria

  • Age: You are 45 years old or above.
  • Homeownership: You own the property where you are considering installing solar.
  • Installation timeframe: You are seriously considering buying or installing solar within the next 20 days.

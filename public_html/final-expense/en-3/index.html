Common questions

Is this a government program?

No. This is private final expense insurance issued by A+ rated, state-licensed carriers and regulated by your state's Department of Insurance.

Do I need a medical exam?

No — just a few short health questions. No physical, no blood draw, no doctor visit.

Will my rate go up over time?

No. Once a policy is issued, the rate is fixed for the life of the policy.

What does it cost?

Rates depend on your age, state, and coverage amount. Many plans start around $1 a day.

    What a roofer looks at

    A roof is a stack of layers. Problems tend to start where two of them meet.

    • ShinglesThe outer layer. Look for lifted, cracked or missing pieces.
    • UnderlaymentA water barrier under the shingles.
    • DeckingThe boards everything fastens to. Soft or dark spots matter.
    • FlashingMetal that seals chimneys, vents and valleys.
    • Attic sideDamp insulation or stained wood can show water getting in.

    Roofers describe slope as rise over run. A 6:12 pitch rises 6 inches for every 12 across.

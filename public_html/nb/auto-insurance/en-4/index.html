Is Your Car Insurance Company Overcharging You?

Your renewal notice tells you what you'll pay next. But it doesn't tell you whether another insurer could offer you a better rate.

By Craig Johnson

For many American drivers, auto insurance has become one more monthly bill putting pressure on the household budget.

You renew your policy. Your premium goes up. And you're left wondering:

“Am I getting a competitive rate—or simply paying what my current insurer asks?”

What many drivers don't realize is that insurance companies calculate rates differently.

Your ZIP code, driving history, vehicle, mileage, and coverage can all affect the rate you're quoted.

And being loyal to the same insurer for years doesn't necessarily mean you're getting the best available rate.

If you've been with the same insurer for more than a year, don't renew until you do this.

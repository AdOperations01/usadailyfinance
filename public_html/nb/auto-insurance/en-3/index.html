Why the Worst Overchargers Are Different in Every ZIP Code

Every insurer uses its own formula. Your ZIP code, driving history, age, vehicle, mileage, prior coverage and claims history all go into it, and each company weighs those factors differently.

The result? The exact same driver can get wildly different rates from different companies. The insurer that's cheapest for your neighbor across town can be the most expensive one for you. That's why there's no single list, and the only way to find out if yours is overcharging you is to compare rates in your ZIP code.

Loyal drivers usually get hit the hardest. Many insurers raise rates a little at every renewal, betting you won't shop around. When drivers compare, many are shocked to find another insurer offering a much lower rate for the same coverage.

Why Now

Rates have climbed for years, and many insurers raise prices again at renewal. Every month you wait is another month of paying more than you have to.

Consumer Reports advises drivers to compare quotes before buying or renewing car insurance. "By shopping periodically, you have the potential to save with your current carrier, or with a new insurer that's just as good or better."
